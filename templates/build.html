Meet the Σ4

A full-octave digital synth has 12 keys, a slide ribbon for pitch, three onboard effects.

Three effects

  • Pitch slide

    Soft spot on A1 lerps the note continuously across the octave

  • Digital volume

    Pot on A2 scales amplitude in firmware

  • Vibrato

    A synthio LFO controls frequency while a key is held. It is toggled from the keypad
